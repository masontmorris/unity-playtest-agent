## Setup

- **Target.** FPSKit development bug `b02`, branch `eval/fpskit-dev/b02`, seed `bc069b5`. The
  development set is retired: the owner and orchestrator may read it and its bugs may drive tuning.
  The agent was not given a key; S1 received the same generic exploration objective as earlier runs
  (objective SHA-256 in Records). The blind eval set was untouched; Day 8 uses it.
- **Seed specification.** A weapon pad's item returns after its respawn delay but remains frozen.
  `PickupDispenser.m_RespawnTTL`, exposed as `public.dispenser_a_timer` and
  `public.dispenser_b_timer`, continues below zero rather than stopping. The gate's recorded finding
  and replay measure the timer condition; they do not independently establish the item's visual state.
- **Preflight.** Fresh on the seeded build. Discovery recorded 312 fields, 26 bindings and 0 errors;
  entity identification succeeded. Calibration was `measured_with_gaps`: 276 trials planned,
  254 recorded, 304.7 s elapsed. Its stepped-clock support was unknown because of runtime errors.
  The 640x480 frame came from a separate one-tap probe session on the same build, not the calibration
  or play session.
- **Manifest.** Stages 4–6 used four Standard `muse-spark-1.3` calls, including one validation retry.
  The manifest has 16 readers, 13 actions, 3 skills and 3 invariants. Offline validation of its schema,
  references, semantics, source packet and recorded answers passed. Stage 7 returned `different` for
  `move_forward`, as on Day 4. The input probe recorded 11/11 action and 3/3 skill successes for the
  named declarations; it measures applied-input receipts, releases and sampled changes, not all game
  effects. `interact` and `primary_action` had no resolved hold and were not among the 11 probed actions.
- **Sessions.** Five sessions had a 600 s wall limit, S1 `liquid-d1-ultra`, frontier
  `muse-spark-1.3` on Standard and recording enabled. Sessions 1–3 ended by `finish` before the wall
  limit; sessions 4–5 ended at `wall_limit`. Unity was `6000.5.4f1`, with realtime clock and
  `input_system` input. The original approval covered a fresh preflight and up to three sessions; the
  extension allowed up to five more on the same terms. Each session's ledger cap was $1.55.

frontier clip exists and its SHA-256 matches. Video alignment is approximate, based on frame-file
completion times rather than capture or game time.

seeded finding; neither was replayed, and whether either is a real defect is unknown.

satisfied the finding's check: 5/5 reproduced, 0 unknown and 0 invalid. Each baseline after scene reload

about 61 s into each replay. Each attempt replayed 15 inputs, with every start within 0.031 s of its
recorded offset. The game git head and reported worktree state were unchanged before and after the
replay. This establishes that the selected check recurred under these replay conditions, not by itself
that the item was visually frozen.

reproduced 5/5, with the frontier clip linked and the evidence timestamp in the session video. The two

## Limits

- The target was from the development set, and four general fixes were made after misses on that target.
  This is not a blind evaluation; the untouched blind set is for Day 8.
- The seeded finding was recorded in one of five sessions, after those changes. The sessions therefore
  do not estimate a fixed-system discovery rate. Two other, unreproduced candidates came from session 5.
- `repro_check` was authored by the frontier. The 5/5 result scores that check, not an independent visual
  assessment or a verified fix.
- Replay used real time and a scene reload between attempts within one PlayMode session, not five
  independent Editor launches. Its samples establish when the check was first observed to hold, not
  the exact time it first became true.
- Calibration ran on the seeded build, so it learned that build's behavior as normal. Its status was
  `measured_with_gaps`; the stage 7 difference and unknown stepped-clock support remain limitations.

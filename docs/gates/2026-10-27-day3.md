# Day 3 gate — Preflight 0–3 and the S1 bake-off

Date: 2026-10-27 (local; 2026-10-28 UTC). Tree: `357d853` (the `main` merge of #101).

Gate criteria from the `Day 3 – Preflight 0–3 + S1 bake-off` milestone:

1. FPSKit entity found by measurement.
2. Freshness classes measured.
3. S1 model chosen on bench evidence.

All three are supported, each with the limits named below. This is a review of recorded evidence: no
probe, trial or test was re-run for it. The local records the notes cite were opened, their SHA-256
hashes checked, and the figures below read from them. CI on `main` at `357d853` passed (`test (3.11)`,
`test (3.13)`, `secrets`, `sync`). Unity tests do not run in CI, so their counts are the ones the probe
notes record.

## 1. Entity found by measurement

Issue #22 (PR #83); note `docs/probes/2026-10-27-fpskit-entity.md`.

- Four discovered keyboard controls (A, D, S, W) were pressed twice each, with a 0.4 s lease, against a
  sampled idle baseline. All 8 executions completed with release verified, the final stop verified
  release, and the largest idle displacement was zero.
- Exactly one input-bound responding entity came out: `FirstPersonController` (`status: identified`,
  within-run id `scene:Assets/FPSKit_TestScene.unity@1::5`). It moved 2.0–2.1 world units in the pressed
  direction in every trial.
- A second root also moved: the Cinemachine camera, which has no discovered binding or physical body. Its
  displacement differed from the entity's by at most 0.025 world units, inside the recorded match
  tolerance (0.03 absolute, 2% relative), so it is classified as the viewpoint and not a second entity.
- The coordinate frame is Unity world space (x, y, z) and the unit is the Unity world unit. A physical
  unit was not measured: `physical_unit` is `null`.
- Discovery was not truncated: 62 objects, 215 components and 26 live bindings, with 4 candidates against
  a cap of 32.
- Disclosed: the first run returned `ambiguous` because the camera and the player both responded. The
  camera comparison was added afterwards and the later runs include it. The evidence is the final run,
  `runs/preflight/issue22-fpskit-05/entity.json` (SHA-256 `6e8a80ca…`, checked).
- Not established: gamepad sticks, mouse delta and legacy input; motion between idle samples; an id that
  persists across runs.

## 2. Freshness classes measured

Issues #23 (PR #84) and #85–#87 (PR #92); notes `docs/probes/2026-10-27-fpskit-calibration.md` and
`docs/probes/2026-10-27-fpskit-freshness.md`.

Two full runs (`runs/preflight/issue85-fpskit-01/record.json` and `-02`, SHA-256 `cb647592…` and
`d1a6fdf1…`, checked) classified all 302 fields in the entity's scene. Every field got the same class in
both runs.

| Class | Fields |
|---|---:|
| Changed at idle and while held | 7 |
| Changed only while held | 4 |
| No change observed | 207 |
| Unknown | 84 |

- Five of the seven changed continuously at idle and under every key, so held input alone does not explain
  them. Two changed at idle and, of the held keys, only under F, Ctrl, Shift and Space. The four
  held-only fields changed only under A, D, S and W.
- Unknown is not unchanged. The 84 are 46 of a type the reader does not serialize, 26 object references,
  11 input actions and 1 that was null at idle and held an unsupported type once input began.
- The runs agreed to within 0.047 world units on any key-and-hold mean displacement. Windows of the same
  scope disagreed for 2 fields in run 01 and 3 in run 02; the record names them in
  `varied_between_windows`. The record's own status is `measured_with_gaps`.
- Sampling is thin: a held window has usually 4 intervals and an idle window 7, 0.17–0.33 s apart.
- Not established: the freshness of the 84 unknown fields; that "no change observed" means constant;
  causes; anything between samples. The classes are not yet attached to actions: the live test's action
  catalog declared no dependent fields, so only age could make an action stale. Stepped mode and reset
  are outside this criterion (see `docs/probes/2026-10-27-fpskit-reset.md`).

## 3. S1 model chosen on bench evidence

Issues #24 and #25 (PRs #96, #98, #99, #101); decision record `docs/design/s1-model.md`.

- `mammoth-1` was chosen on 27 October, provisionally. On the same 40 frames and the same transport,
  against accept-lists a person reviewed, it was acceptable on 40/40, against elephant-1's 30/40 and
  liquid-d1.1's 21/40. Neither beat it on any frame. Adjacent repeats flipped the top choice 0 of 40
  times for mammoth-1 and elephant-1 and 14 of 40 for liquid-d1.1.
- It held on a shuffled re-test (45/45) and in the production turn loop, live: 3 episodes, 30 decisions,
  each episode checked all 8 controls once and finished. The live trace (SHA-256 `43cdf80e…`, checked)
  records adherence 1.0 with no violations in all three episodes. Its 3 stale rejections were recorded and
  nothing was substituted.
- Method: designs predeclared in `docs/design/s1-bench.md`, a recorded approval and a per-call ledger for
  every paid call, a noise measurement, and paired per-frame scoring with no pooled claim.
- Limits: the labels were reviewed at the level of the rule, after the results were seen, with no second
  labeller. One game and one bookkeeping task. Jev and Liquid's d1 and d1-ultra were not run.
- The choice is provisional on three open conditions, which this gate does not ask to settle: the price
  after the trial (unknown; the credit request in #8 has had no reply), latency at full context size
  (median 875 ms at about 13,200 tokens against the loop's 0.5 s freshness window), and a rate limit
  (HTTP 429 after 23 calls). #100 revisits them on Day 5.

## Verdict

Pass. The entity was identified by measured displacement against an idle baseline, the freshness classes
were measured with the unknown fields kept unknown, and the S1 model was chosen on a paired, predeclared
bench with its open conditions written down. Issue #8 stays open in this milestone; no gate criterion
depends on it.
